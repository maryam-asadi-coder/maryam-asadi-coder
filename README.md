## 🌍 About Me

I'm a **Climate & Environmental Data Scientist** with an academic background in **Civil Engineering and Water Resources**, working at the intersection of:

* 🌎 **Climate & Environmental Data Science**
* 🌊 **Water Resources & Hydrological Systems**
* 🛰️ **Remote Sensing & Earth Observation**
* 📍 **Geospatial Data Science & Machine Learning**
* 🤖 **AI / ML for Environmental Systems**
* ⚡ **Climate, Energy & Water-Energy-Food Nexus**

My work focuses on transforming complex environmental and geospatial data into **data-driven insights, predictive models, and practical solutions** for climate and environmental challenges.

I work across the full data workflow, from **data acquisition and preprocessing** to **exploratory analysis, geospatial processing, machine learning, visualization, and model evaluation**.
